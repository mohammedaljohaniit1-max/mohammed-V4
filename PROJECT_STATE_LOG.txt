2. Surgical Precision & Low Impact (Target-Safe Governor):

3. High-Impact Target Identification (No "Trivial" Bugs):

4. Strict False-Positive Elimination (Validation Layer):
   - Dynamic 404 detection (soft 404 / custom error page fingerprinting).
   - Dual-probe verification (two distinct signatures required before flagging).
   - Exact banner & checksum validation for confirmed vulnerable signatures.
